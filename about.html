About

Practical AI, without the fog machine.

AIFlowGuide is an independent educational site focused on practical AI tools, automation, prompting, and productivity systems.

The editorial goal is straightforward: explain what a tool does, show where it fits, identify limitations, and make the result usable by a normal person with a normal budget.

Editorial principles

  • Original writing and practical examples.
  • Clear distinction between facts, estimates, and opinions.
  • Primary documentation for technical claims when available.
  • Human review for important decisions and outputs.

Monetization

This site may use advertising or affiliate relationships in the future. Any commercial relationship will not change our commitment to disclose relevant relationships and keep content useful.
