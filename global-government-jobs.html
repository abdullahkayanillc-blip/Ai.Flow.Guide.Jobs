Global application reality

The job title is only half the question.

For cross-border roles, eligibility can depend on citizenship, right-to-work status, security requirements, professional registration, language skills, location and willingness to relocate. Always read the exact vacancy notice.

Application strategy

How to compete without gaming the search engine

Match the notice

Mirror the real competency language in your CV when it accurately reflects your experience. Do not fabricate skills.

Track deadlines

Keep a simple spreadsheet of vacancy, source URL, closing date, eligibility notes, submitted date and reference number.

Verify the source

Search for the same vacancy on the official agency or government domain before sharing money, identity documents or sensitive details.
